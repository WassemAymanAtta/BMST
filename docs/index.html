Who we are

More than a team. A community.

BMST is a volunteer-led sports team in Bani Mazar. We use the game we love to create opportunities for young people and their families.

  • A safe place to play and belong
  • Coaching built on respect and discipline
  • Real service projects in our neighbourhood
  • A path from player to leader
